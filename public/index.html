            About MyTunes Redux Reborn

            Version 2.2.1 “Standalone”

            A modern, legitimate reimagining of the classic myTunes Redux experience for browsing and downloading from DAAP-compatible music shares you are authorized to access.

            Disclaimer: MyTunes Redux Reborn is an independent open-source reimplementation of the historical myTunes Redux project by Bill Zeller. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or related to mytunes.ai, Apple Inc., or iTunes.

            In memory of Bill Zeller

            This project is dedicated to Bill Zeller, creator of the original myTunes and myTunes Redux. His curiosity about how iTunes sharing worked, and his willingness to build tools that made network music sharing usable, inspired a generation of developers and music lovers. Thank you, Bill.

            How this was created

            This initial recreation was produced through reverse-engineering analysis of the original installer and application using REA (Reverse Engineering Anything), followed by a clean-room modern reimplementation. Shout-out to the REA tooling and to Beam Free for assisting with the analysis and rebuild planning.

            DAAP authentication in iTunes 7+ / Home Sharing remains proprietary; this client supports standards-compatible and legacy shares where permitted.
